Animesh Jha

I recently completed my MS in CS from Stanford, where I worked with Prof. Sanmi Koyejo on Machine Unlearning.

I interned at Cartesia AI, working with their Serving Infrastructure team. Previously, I was a Software Engineer at Rubrik (Core Infra Team). I completed my B.Tech in CSE at IIT Kharagpur in 2023.

My research interests lie in questions around efficiency, privacy, and robustness of systems. I have had a lot of fun exploring Optimization, Distributed Learning, Differential Privacy, Compilers, and Robotics through my various research experiences.
